What I do in paid search

Before the first euro is spent, I audit GA4 and Google Tag Manager, then set up the conversion events that matter to you: form submitted, data sheet downloaded, click on an email address or phone number. These conversions are imported into Google Ads.

Then I build the campaigns:

  • Search: responsive search ads, ad assets, and a landing page matched to each ad group (I create the missing ones).
  • Remarketing: audiences built from site visitors and the product pages they viewed.
  • Display, Shopping and Video when the product suits them.

Every week, I review conversions by campaign and move budget and bids toward what produces leads. Results go into a Looker Studio dashboard you can open whenever you want.

I have also run Meta Ads campaigns (Facebook and Instagram) for Natural Beauty Store.
