What I do in SEO

Every project starts with an audit. I run the site through SEMrush and Google Search Console to see which pages are indexed, which ones report crawl errors and which queries the site already appears for.

Then I work in this order:

  1. Keyword research and competitor page analysis, to pick the queries that bring in customers and not just traffic.
  2. Technical: indexing, crawl errors, sitemap, page speed (Core Web Vitals).
  3. On-page: title tags and meta descriptions, H1 and H2 structure, content of the target pages, alt attributes, internal linking.
  4. Content: new pages and articles for the queries the site does not cover yet.
  5. Link building: getting links from other sites to the target pages.

I track rankings, clicks and impressions in Search Console and in a Looker Studio dashboard.
