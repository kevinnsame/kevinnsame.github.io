SEO case study: #1 on Google for 4 keywords for AMADA Europe

The European website of AMADA, a sheet metal machinery manufacturer, reached first position on Google for four generic queries typed by industrial buyers. Here is how.

Context

AMADA Europe sells sheet metal machinery: press brakes, laser cutting machines, punching machines. The European website is in English and runs on Typo3. I worked there as a digital marketing specialist on a work-study contract from September 2023 to January 2025.

A buyer looking for a new machine rarely starts with a brand name. They type the kind of machine they need. The goal was to show up on these generic searches, against other manufacturers and resellers.

Goal

Rank the site for four strategic queries, one per main product family:

  • Bending Machine
  • Laser Cutting Machine
  • Punching Machine
  • Sheet Metal

Approach

1. Keyword research and competitor analysis

I studied the industry's search queries and competing pages in SEMrush, then selected the four target keywords.

2. Technical foundations

A page Google cannot crawl properly will not rank, even with good content. I worked on indexing, fixed the crawl errors reported in Search Console, kept the sitemap up to date and monitored page speed.

3. On-page optimization

  • Title tags and meta descriptions rewritten around each page's keyword
  • Clearer H1 and H2 structure
  • Product page content optimized for the target keywords
  • More internal links pointing to the target pages
  • Alt attributes added to images

4. Content and links

I created new pages and articles, and worked on link building to get links from other sites to the target pages.

Result

All four target keywords reached first position on Google: Bending Machine, Laser Cutting Machine, Punching Machine and Sheet Metal.

Tools used

SEMrush, Google Search Console, Typo3.
