Google Ads case study: +47% leads in 2 months for Castles Technology

At Castles Technology, a payment terminal manufacturer, the Google Ads campaigns I set up increased the number of leads by 47% in two months. Measurement mattered as much as the ads.

Context

Castles Technology designs and sells payment terminals to businesses. For the sales team, a lead is a visitor who downloads a data sheet, sends an email or calls. I joined the company as digital marketing specialist in September 2025.

Step 1: measure before spending

Before launching any campaign, I audited the site: technical performance (Core Web Vitals) and the GA4 setup. I then created GA4 conversion events for the actions the sales team cares about:

  • data sheet download
  • click on an email address
  • click on a phone number

These conversions were then imported into Google Ads. Without them, there is no way to know which campaign or ad produces leads, or where to adjust bids. I also set up Google Search Console to track organic visibility in parallel.

Step 2: Search campaigns

I wrote the responsive search ads and added ad assets, then chose the most relevant landing page for each ad, or created the ones that were missing. An ad that promises a specific terminal should lead to that terminal's page, not to the home page.

Step 3: remarketing

A business buyer rarely visits a site only once before getting in touch. I built remarketing audiences from site visitors and the product pages they viewed, to reach them again with matching ads while they were still deciding.

Step 4: weekly optimization

Once the campaigns were live, I reviewed conversions by campaign every week and shifted bids and budget toward what produced leads. I track results in Looker Studio dashboards.

Result

In two months, the number of leads rose by 47%.

Tools used

Google Ads, Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, Looker Studio, Google Search Console.
